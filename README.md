## PCB Projects

See all three in browser (schematic, layout, 3D render, BOM): 
**[laaaarry.github.io](https://laaaarry.github.io/)**

**[STM32L496RG breakout](https://github.com/Laaaarry/STM32L496RG_Breakout)**:  4 layers, 57
components, two internal ground planes. Buffered I²C, CAN, and other breakouts. Brought up with an ST-LINK and verified on the scope: PWM at 2 kHz / 50 % duty, a clean I²C START-address-NACK, and
CAN loopback with RX mirroring TX.

**[Onboard computer](https://github.com/Laaaarry/Onboard-Computer-PCB)**: 2 layers, 32 components. Simple OBC featuring the STM32F103, with buffered I²C, external oscillator, and power circuitry. Programmable through STLink. The first board I took from schematic to a working blink.

**[3V3 buck converter](https://github.com/Laaaarry/3V3-Buck-Converter-PCB)**: 3V3 buck around an LMR50410. Target was under 100 mV output ripple; measured 36 mV pk-pk. First ever PCB I made.
